# Current GATr training and unused data — September 17, 2026

Audit of the current H100 physical snapshot and onset runs, tracing the actual
trainer, target producer, cohort and sampled-row logs. The local dataset registry
and selected completion records were inspected; archived arrays were not all
rehash-verified and the remote H200 server was not contacted.
[Machine-readable evidence and log hashes](gatr_20260917.json).

## Current training population and observations

The verified cohort contains 150 independently melted Al sources: 30 each at
400, 450, 500, 510 and 520 K. Each contains 70,304 atoms and 801 frames spanning
0–600 ps at 0.75 ps cadence. The three collections contribute 90 original
400–500 K sources, 36 original 510–520 K sources and 24 completed 520 K recovery
sources. The September 13 inventory's 126-complete count is outdated here.

The fixed grid uses 16 tracked centers/source, selected from an outcome-independent
64-center pool, and 16 anchors/center: 48, 78, ..., 498 ps. The encoder receives
center-relative positions and velocities within 17 Angstrom, with a smooth
15–17 Angstrom boundary. Many atoms contribute to each neighborhood: 16 tracked
centers does not mean 16 input atoms. Boxes establish the periodic local chart;
atom IDs establish correspondence and are not numerical learned features.

Snapshot observes one frame. History observes 17 frames from t−12 ps through t,
at 0.75 ps spacing. Repeated-frame controls repeat current positions, velocities
and membership over those offsets. For each center, the candidate snapshot grid
covers 16 input frames; history covers 272 distinct frames. Future frames are
targets only. Other frames can contribute to event-label construction; sparse
encoder inputs do not mean the remaining trajectory has never been read.

GATr has two spatial/temporal block pairs, eight multivector and 128 scalar
channels. Snapshot bypasses temporal blocks; causal history blocks start with
zero temporal gates when initialized from their own snapshot parent. Geometric
inputs encode positions/velocities; scalar inputs are support, squared radius,
squared speed, signed radial motion and smooth count. There is no species
channel: current runs are pure Al. The current center's scalar features project
to z128; GATr has no final multiscale atom pooling.
[Encoder implementation](../../src/models/encoders/axial_gatr.py).

## Losses and optimization

**No VICReg is used in current GATr runs.** The v2 trainer never instantiates the
earlier VICReg module. No paired-view similarity, latent variance floor,
covariance decorrelation, whitening, bending or slowness objective is active.
Optimizer weight decay is not VICReg. Architectural geometric symmetry is
distinct from learning invariance through paired augmented views.

The physical snapshot model starts from fresh random weights and minimizes:

`MSE(present physical packet) + mean_lag MSE(future physical packet)`.

The six future lags are 0.75, 3, 9, 24, 48 and 96 ps. Both terms have coefficient
one; channels, not blocks, are equally weighted within each MSE. The fixed
128-channel packet comprises 32 radial, 32 pair-distance, 16 angular, 16 speed,
16 radial-velocity and 16 mixed geometric/motion moments on smooth 5–7 Angstrom
support. Normalization uses all candidate training-source anchors and their
six futures, independently of crystal labels.

The present head is a 128→128→128 SiLU MLP. A linear future head maps z128 plus
seven conditions to 6×128 outputs. Conditions are five temperature indicators,
t/600 and (t/600)^2, standardized on training rows. They enter the prediction
head, not the atom features. All encoder/head weights train jointly.

Optimizer: AdamW, lr=3e−4, weight decay=1e−4, gradient norm clipping=5, FP32 with
TF32 disabled, effective/microbatch eight, seed 20260919, 2,048 updates. Sampling
draws a source uniformly with replacement, then a window uniformly from that
source; sources can repeat within one batch. Selection uses 64 fixed rows per
selection source. The separate 32-window fitting gate is diagnostic: its weights
do not initialize the broad physical fit. Neither pretrained nor VICReg weights
initialize the model.

The onset runs train **different encoders**. A fresh onset-only snapshot parent
trains for 4,096 updates. Its selected checkpoint initializes three separate
4,096-update continuations: snapshot, history12 and repeat12. Their sole task
loss is six-bin event/survival negative log-likelihood, with a linear z+conditions
head. Physical reconstruction and TDA are absent. The risk set requires no earlier
sustained onset and no crystalline center in the three latest frames. This is
not fine-tuning the physical encoder. The same sampler is reset for parent and
continuations; their logged anchor/center draw sequences match exactly.

The TDA experiment freezes the **physical snapshot** encoders and trains only
new readouts. Its nearest-80 topology targets do not update encoder weights, and
its results do not measure topology retained by the onset-only checkpoints.

The registry includes potentials, fixtures, failed/superseded attempts and copies.
Entries outside the cohort in the JSON are not a count of ready independent
datasets. The older 72-replica directory is prepared-only. The 100k Al collection
has a stale `running` status without a completed canonical release; this audit
does not promote it to ready data. See the [historical inventory](../simulations/inventory_20260913.md)
and [current precision campaign record](../simulations/predictive_memory_precision_20260917.md).

The immediate expansion is more windows and centers from the existing training
lineages, preserving all held-out sources. The three dense precision sources can
support a separate temporal-resolution/quantization comparison. Static data and
shooting branches need separate, protocol-aware incorporation. This audit changes
no training code, split, job or loss.
