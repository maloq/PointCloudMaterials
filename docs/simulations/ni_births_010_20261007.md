## Interpreting the eleven-record count: October 8 audit

Eleven is the number of liquid-origin root families passing the 64-atom/1.5-ps
rule, not the total number of growing crystal histories or crystallizing runs.
**All six 1100 K runs develop large crystals**, including melt03, whose growth
is classified through subcritical coalescence rather than an independently
established root. Their final native-coordinate PTM crystal fractions are
47.7–73.1%. The largest stored-coordinate clusters exceed 15,000 atoms in every
1100 K run. At 1200, 1300 and 1400 K, maxima across all six trajectories at each
temperature are only 13, 10 and 5 crystalline atoms, respectively. These high
temperature negative results are already present before lineage tracking.

At 1100 K the PTM catalogue additionally contains four established near-interface
roots, four established roots with unresolved crystal connection, and 32
established composite records. Only four of those composites establish without
an inherited established crystal. Sequential composites can describe the same
growing crystal, so summing these categories does not yield a physical birth count.
Existing liquid-root sensitivity fields give 18 records at 32 atoms/1.5 ps,
11 at 64 atoms/1.5 ps, six at 128 atoms/1.5 ps and eight at 64 atoms/3 ps. All occur
at 1100 K. Short threshold crossings and uncertain identities need separate
interpretation; these operational records do not estimate a nucleation rate.

The independent execution cross-check uses the same PTM classifier on different
coordinate precision and cadence: native simulation monitoring every 1 ps versus
postprocessing of saved float16 coordinates every 0.1 ps. At 11,060 common
observations, only two disagree on whether the largest component reaches 64
atoms. Neither comparison reveals a missed large crystal at 1200–1400 K.
This supports the large-scale crystallization contrast; it is not an exhaustive
validation of each small-embryo lineage or a proof of physical criticality.
The saved audit is `ni-20261007-24-v1/technical/nucleation-count-audit-20261008.json`
under the search result storage root.

Eighteen of 24 runs and approximately 85% of saved simulation time were spent
at 1200–1400 K, where this finite observation window yielded no established
crystalline families. A finer prospective temperature screen near 1100 K and
between 1100–1200 K would target the observed transition more effectively.
Prestructured liquid is a separate structural target and is not exhausted by
counting PTM-crystalline births. No thresholds, simulations or catalogue entries
were changed by this audit.

## Physical protocol

Twelve periodic cells contain 32,000 Ni atoms each: three separate stochastic
melts for each of 1100, 1200, 1300 and 1400 K. Temperatures are an exploratory
undercooling screen, not an optimized rate window. The unchanged
`Ni1_Mendelev_2012.eam.fs` author file is hash pinned. [NIST's potential record](https://www.ctcms.nist.gov/potentials/entry/2012--Mendelev-M-I-Kramer-M-J-Hao-S-G-et-al--Ni/)
describes its intended liquid/melting application. A potential-specific melting
point is not remeasured by this campaign.

Each source has its own velocity/preparation seed and full-cell melt, with no
replicated liquid blocks, nucleus seeding or selected-nucleus restarts. The
three preparation roles are frozen as train, selection and test before running;
the four temperatures within each role also have independent melts. All are
retained regardless of whether births occur. No predictive model is trained.

- Native LAMMPS isotropic NPT at zero external pressure, with GPU EAM forces.
- Timestep **1 fs**; thermostat/barostat damping **0.1/1.0 ps**.
- Start from a 20x20x20 conventional FCC cell, lattice parameter 3.68 Angstrom,
  then melt at **2300 K for 40 ps**. The initial lattice is only a preparation seed.
- Require MSD of at least **10 Angstrom²** over the last 20 ps, PTM crystal fraction
  at most **2%**, and largest crystal component below **32 atoms**, before cooling.
  A failed preparation is reported; its crystal is never relabelled as liquid.
- Save the complete **20 ps cooling ramp** and subsequent **up-to-500 ps hold**,
  at **0.10 ps / 100 integration steps**, starting with the validated liquid.
- Inspect native-coordinate PTM structure every 1 ps. Stop 10 ps after the first
  observation at or above 50% crystal, and at least 10 ps after cooling ends.
  Otherwise retain the entire no-birth/right-censored trajectory to 500 ps.

The first source order is 1200, 1100, 1300, 1400 K, then the next independent
replicate. Cooling births and constant-temperature births remain separate in
both observables and the event catalogue.

## Engine and precision

The force engine is the validated [FastNi CUDA EAM](ni_eam_gpu_20261007.md).
The new adapter retains LAMMPS's native Nose-Hoover thermostat/barostat, float64
positions/velocities and restart precision. Force and force-derivative arithmetic
is float32; total energy and virial reductions are float64. Every callback checks
actual atom IDs and the displacement from affinely transformed list-build
coordinates. Rebuild when `s*(cutoff+skin) - 2*max_displacement` approaches the
force cutoff, where `s` is the smallest cell stretch since building the list.
This lower bound protects against omitted pairs entering the cutoff under both
motion and cell compression. Reordering also triggers a rebuild. The
adapter supplies the correct scalar virial trace for **isotropic** pressure;
individual normal stresses and shear stresses are not physical outputs.

NPT throughput includes host/native integration and differs from the earlier
GPU-resident NVE benchmark. A production-size native-EAM versus GPU-EAM audit
checks force, energy, scalar pressure, 100-step NPT trajectory and volume before
production is submitted. A separate end-to-end observation check verifies Q/V,
integer timeline, quantization, checksums and all four structural detectors.
These validation cells are excluded from production ancestry and birth counts.

Following the October 7 user request, numerical validation is reused across
campaigns, allocations and independent seeds. The reviewed receipts in
`configs/simulation/ni_births_validation.json` bind the potential, numerical
settings and hashes of the MD adapter, CUDA force implementation and Q/V
conversion/publication. `submit-audit` reuses a compatible receipt without
submitting a job; production records it in `technical/validation-reuse.json`.
An allocation attachment checks its actual frozen producer and GPU type against
the saved evidence before changing queue ownership, then starts production
without another numerical run. New or changed implementations require an
explicit audit and review of the catalogue entry. Existing receipts are retained.
The current catalogue contains the passed A40 and Blackwell checks; the earlier
L40S check predates the publication cleanup correction. This change affects
future launches only; running queues have already completed their relevant
audits and do not validate each trajectory. Per-source liquid preparation gates and per-chunk
conversion/checksum verification continue because they check newly produced data.

Observation chunks are first captured from native float64 Q/V without a text
dump, then converted through:

```bash
python scripts/convert_trajectory.py native-qv INPUT.npz OUTPUT --delete-source
```

The output is the existing shooting-binary schema: float16 positions and
velocities, float32 box bounds, exact int64 steps/IDs and int32 types. Velocities
are **Angstrom/ps**. Conversion measures errors against every native float64
observation and verifies decoded payloads/checksums before removing its NPZ.
Native LAMMPS restart files are retained every 5 ps. Observation precision is
never used for integration or native recovery.

Completed chunks are composed into a single standard binary trajectory on
STORE with an exact contiguous timeline, then verified. Only verified temporary
observation chunks from this campaign are removed. Native checkpoints, failed
preparations and partial failures are archived. Interrupted native runs require
an explicit recovery decision, never an automatic fresh melt under the same ID.

## Birth search

A CPU job follows MD automatically. It includes only completed production
sources and reports failures, partial and not-started sources in coverage.
It reuses the existing full-cell structural extractor and v3 descendant-family
tracker without altering any historical Al definitions or outputs.

Ni-specific geometry: **3.2 Angstrom** neighbor cutoff and **7.0 Angstrom**
interface proximity. These approximately scale the Al first-shell/proximity
supports by Ni's smaller lattice length. They are declared prospective settings,
not fitted to observed event counts. PTM uses RMSD 0.10; crystalline PTM labels
are FCC/HCP/BCC. Three additional q6 coherence definitions use the unchanged
(.65,6), (.70,7), (.75,8) bond/solid-neighbor thresholds.

Components start tracking at four atoms; embryo appearance is eight atoms.
Establishment requires a connected descendant of at least **64 atoms for 1.5 ps**;
32/128-atom and 1.5/3 ps crossing fields are retained. Splits preserve families;
coalescence is recorded separately and does not invent additional births.
Initial embryos, insufficient prehistory, proximity to existing crystals,
absorption, dissolution and end-of-observation censoring remain explicit.

These are **operational Ni embryo/establishment labels**, not a measured critical
nucleus size or committor. Multiple detectors, descendants and events within a
cell do not create additional independent preparations. Temperature and phase
are audit fields, not encoder/predictor inputs.
