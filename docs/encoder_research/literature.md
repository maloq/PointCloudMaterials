The central implication of the literature is to separate **information retained in a representation, the geometry of its coordinates, the law generated by a predictor, and the physical event being estimated**. Success at one does not establish the others. Recent work strengthens these distinctions; it does not establish that a particular mechanism explains this repository or authorize a new architecture queue.

- **M — methods checked:** relevant primary-paper methods, theoretical assumptions, or results passages were inspected during this review sequence. This does not mean every proof, supplement, or numerical result was independently verified.

- **Repository finding** identifies existing evidence; **working hypothesis** identifies an untested explanation; **candidate** identifies conditional work. None is a claim of novelty to the wider literature.

## 1. Representations: what should be retained, and where?

### 1.1 Kinetic objectives answer a particular future question

**GDyNet** is the closest established atomic precedent: Xie et al. combine local graph features with a VAMP objective and interpret interfacial states through their dynamics. Its Si–Au example is relevant to local solid/liquid/interface organization, but its chosen targets and state count define the question. It does not establish a universal liquid metric or demonstrate prospective Al nucleus prediction. Its code uses atomic species and full periodic graphs, whereas new native PointCloudMaterials encoders retain the constant-channel geometry-only contract. **M/C.** [Paper][gdynet-paper]; implementation comparison in section 6.

**VAMP and VAMPnets** optimize features for approximating lagged dynamics through singular functions and whitened cross-covariance. Wu and Noé explicitly allow nonreversible and nonstationary processes/realizations; rejecting VAMP merely because trajectories are nonequilibrium would be incorrect. The separate issue is whether powers of one pooled operator represent a homogeneous law for the chosen observation and protocol. Cooling, projection, finite features, and unequal lag populations complicate that interpretation. **M.** [VAMP theory][vamp]; [VAMPnets][vampnets].

**State-free reversible VAMPnets** offer continuous slow functions instead of a prescribed softmax partition; **kinetic maps** give a specified future-distribution meaning to distance under their assumptions. **SPIB** compresses observations while predicting future states, with delay controlling resolution. These are valuable alternatives to “seven colors are seven states,” but reversibility/equilibrium assumptions and chosen future labels matter. A compression that preserves the selected kinetics can discard interface morphology or fast rearrangements that another scientific question needs. **M for the cited method passages.** [SRV][srv]; [kinetic maps][kinetic-maps]; [SPIB][spib].

Qiu et al. (2025) show that short-time structural fluctuations in a Kob–Andersen liquid can expose much slower relaxation propensity. Medium-range and species-resolved structure matter in that study. The transferable idea is to test short-lag predictive information, not to import its descriptors, lags, or autoencoding treatment into metals. Physical-reconstruction encoder pretraining remains retired here. **M.** [PNAS study][qiu].

**Repository correction.** Linear VAMP and conditional-future-law learning were already tried. The historical linear VAMP comparison lost to static PCA on the harder matched assay; the existing estimator already uses stabilized covariance calculations. This is evidence against calling kinetic training untried, not a theorem against every kinetic objective. The September representation ledger explicitly corrected its original recommendation after the mechanism audit. [Historical correction][representation-ledger]; [completed mechanism evidence][mechanisms]. **Decision:** H3/S4 use bounded readouts and appropriate direct-lag comparisons first; new kinetic encoder training belongs only in E9 after external-future utility is demonstrated.

### 1.2 Predictive information, distance, and a discrete partition are different objects

An invertible change of coordinates can preserve information while changing Euclidean distances. A trained nonlinear readout, nearest-neighbor retrieval, K-means partition, and density landscape therefore ask different questions. Kinetic maps supply one behavior-defined distance; they do not make every learned Euclidean metric physical. Locatello et al. show why unsupervised disentanglement also requires assumptions rather than visual interpretation of latent axes. **M/A as recorded in the retained ledger.** [Kinetic maps][kinetic-maps]; [disentanglement][locatello].

**VICReg** controls paired agreement, coordinate spread, and cross-covariance. Those objectives do not certify retention of a specified distinction within the liquid stratum. Its official code places the objective after a projector and evaluates the backbone; native MACE instead regularizes the exported 128-dimensional state directly. The distinction is a measured implementation difference, not evidence that adding a projector must improve the native model. **M/C.** [VICReg paper][vicreg-paper]; [official objective][vicreg-code]; [native objective][native-objective].

Feature suppression and dimensional-collapse studies explain plausible failures of self-supervision. Robinson et al. investigate contrastive shortcuts; Jing et al. analyze low-dimensional collapse. Papyan et al.'s neural-collapse result concerns terminal supervised classification. Their mechanisms justify conditional spectra and controlled readouts, but none directly diagnoses Epi/VICReg on these atomic pairs. Low rank can describe a useful physical manifold; high rank can coexist with loss of the one distinction that matters. **M/A, retained literature.** [Shortcuts][shortcuts]; [dimensional collapse][collapse]; [neural collapse][neural-collapse].

Dietrich and Salvalaglio (2025) emphasize that learned-coordinate free-energy surfaces depend on the trained map and its geometry. This reinforces preserving fitted transformations and distinguishing a coordinate density from a physical barrier. It does not make a non-equilibrium UMAP density a free-energy estimate. The original ledger screened the author synopsis; this refresh verified the publication and primary indexed discussion, not the complete derivation. **A.** [JCP article][fes].

**Repository finding and unfinished question.** The spatial VICReg report finds informative continuous liquid readouts alongside almost one-color global liquid partitions, and explicitly leaves the liquid-only clusterer unlaunched. H8 is that unfinished question, not a newly discovered literature proposal. E6 separates fit population, export, and metric at fixed K/maturity. One train-fitted diagonal transform must be shared across population cells if that contrast is to isolate population. Continuous readouts remain finite tests of accessibility, not mathematical information ceilings. [Spatial results][spatial-results]; [fit/readout producer][spatial-eval].

### 1.3 Pair construction and symmetry define what invariance means

InfoMin and augmentation-identifiability work make useful invariance conditional on preserving relevant content. VICRegL distinguishes local and global visual objectives. These are reasons to inspect the actual positive pairs and layer being exported; image correspondence is not a justification for making neighboring atomic environments identical. Relaxation, strain, occlusion, and stochastic evolution are not automatically nuisance transformations. **M/A, retained sources.** [InfoMin][infomin]; [augmentation identifiability][content-style]; [VICRegL][vicregl].

Pozdnyakov et al. provide explicit collisions for finite many-body atomic descriptors. This supports a precise capacity concern, not the claim that rotational invariance inherently removes internal angles. Native MACE carries equivariant tensors before its even scalar export, which can retain angular structure while intentionally excluding pseudoscalar handedness. **M/C.** [Completeness paper][completeness]; [native MACE][native-mace].

The historical spatial recipe can represent signed chirality, yet independently mirrors paired views. Its neighbor-centered observation is the nearest 80 points within a preserved 128-point pool. Native graph construction instead consumes already centered coordinates, with periodic-image reconstruction upstream. These producer facts narrow the interpretation of any alignment effect. Changing the coordinate origin of the same patch is a symmetry; selecting a new center and changing support is a different observation. **C.** [Spatial pair construction][spatial-data]; [recipe][spatial-recipe]; [configuration][spatial-config]; [native graph][native-graph].

**New October 2026 addition — Toso et al., “Preserving Unstable Modes Through Inverse Dynamics in JEPA World Models,” v1, 6 October. M.** The paper constructs linear controlled systems where next-step prediction plus SIGReg can discard unstable directions. Exact action reconstruction gives a conditional injectivity result on a reachable subspace; the theory assumes an observable/stabilizable linear setting, and its long-horizon spectral claim needs additional growth conditions. Experiments concern visual control, including trained projectors over frozen backbones. This is a sharper reason to measure task-relevant retained directions than global anti-collapse statistics. It does **not** establish unstable-mode loss in cooling metals or justify importing an action-reconstruction loss. Here its useful transfer is H2/H7's observation-versus-compression diagnostic and response-direction coverage in E4; existing reconstruction restrictions remain unchanged. [Checked theory and experiments][toso].

**Working hypothesis:** the repository's alignment cost may interact with observed/relaxed input domain. The quality producer does not currently cross static and predictive domains identically, while pooled native normalization is calibrated from hot training views. Those are concrete motivations for H7/E5, not proof of domain causation. Comparable same-row outcomes and matched readouts precede a new normalization or direct-versus-projector treatment. [Quality producer][quality-code]; [native preparation][native-pretrain].

## 2. Stochastic dynamics: score the intended law and retained state

### 2.1 Conditional transitions, equilibrium proposals, and path laws

Predictive-state ideas group histories by their conditional futures; bisimulation emphasizes task-dependent behavioral equivalence. They help define what a compressed state should preserve. They do not prove that a local 80-atom observation is Markovian, or that one binary event label identifies all future behavior. Local causal-state work on discrete spacetime systems is a conceptual precedent, not a ready atomic implementation. **Retained conceptual sources.** [Computational mechanics][comp-mech]; [local causal states][local-causal]; [bisimulation][bisimulation].

Timewarp learns transferable molecular proposals combined with MCMC targeting equilibrium. Accepted equilibrium samples are valuable, but acceptance does not certify that their sequence has the physical kinetics of the native simulator. TITO-style finite-lag generation instead makes a conditional transition/rollout claim. Both demand exact time and state semantics, and neither makes internal flow-integration time a physical trajectory. **A/C for Timewarp; C for the inspected TITO pipeline.** [Timewarp paper][timewarp-paper]; [TITO sampler][tito-model].

**Repository finding:** current Ni held-out reporting evaluates horizon/block marginals; joint280 training/selection is a different distinction. Independent future-time particle relabeling can preserve those marginals while changing paths. The planned sensitivity control preserves the disjoint energy-score pair groups as well as each time's means, variances, and samples. This is an analytical property of the score/producer, not a measured finding that Ni dynamics are wrong. H1/E2 must examine complete emitted paths and at least two uncertain future times. Subtracting a fixed observed anchor remains an endpoint transform. [Ni evaluator][ni-eval]; [methods](methods.md).

### 2.2 Proper scores are necessary but not interchangeable

Pic, Dombry, Naveau and Taillardat (2025) formalize proper scores formed by fixed transformations and aggregation. This supports small, interpretable joint-observable or increment scores, while clarifying that non-injective transformations may not identify the full law. Scheuerer and Hamill's variogram score targets dependence but has blind spots; it should complement marginal checks. Neither paper says one scalar ranking certifies a stochastic simulator. **M.** [Aggregation/transformation][pic]; [variogram score][variogram].

For H1/S1, keep distinct the score of an actual finite Gaussian mixture and a Monte Carlo estimate of a latent-law score. The repository's disjoint-pair energy estimator is unbiased under independent particles; an all-pairs estimator is a variance/cost option, not a correction of a demonstrated bias. More particles, repeated RNG keys, and more independent physical sources reduce different uncertainties. These are estimator distinctions in [methods](methods.md), not external performance claims.

A second analytical finding concerns squared error after averaging stochastic predictions: at fixed target and independent draws, its expectation contains mean error plus predictive-mean variance divided by particle count. The current four-particle value loss therefore need not be pure population-mean supervision. Independent emission variance is a separate contribution, so apparently acceptable marginals could coexist with a changed allocation of temporal variability. Whether this occurs materially remains unmeasured. **Decision:** E2/E4 measure it before E9 changes the loss; richer path scores must be applied to emitted trajectories, not only decoded means. [Current objective][ni-dense].

### 2.3 Memory and derivative response require a matched observation

Mori–Zwanzig formulations motivate explicit memory after projection. The inspected LANL implementation supplies useful finite-memory baselines, while its illustrative script centers a full trajectory before splitting and gives methods different observed initialization prefixes. Its autonomous mean predictor also suppresses an unresolved stochastic term. Transfer the memory comparison with train-only transformations and common forecast origins, not the example's exact evaluation choices. **C.** [Core implementation][mz-core]; [example][mz-example].

A fixed history reversal followed by refitting is an invertible feature permutation, not evidence about order dependence. A recurrence composed with itself is not an empirical Chapman–Kolmogorov test. Separately fitted direct-lag and composed estimates require compatible anchor/intermediate populations and an appropriate homogeneity claim; shared data do not make them statistically independent. These deductions explain H3/S4 and E3's controls. GDyNet provides the separate direct-lag operator pattern, but its time/atom splitting is not this project's ancestor uncertainty. [GDyNet operators][gdynet-vamp].

Czarnecki et al.'s Sobolev training uses derivatives of the same target function with respect to the model's input. It does not establish that a fixed-velocity, changed-bath physical intervention equals the derivative of a conditional future mean given a compressed native observation. H2 first checks producer agreement and whether the physical directions survive the observation and compression. **M/A, primary scope checked.** [Sobolev training][sobolev].

Response of a mean can also miss changes to variance, escape, or other distributional functionals. These motivate S2, while S3 keeps actual full-cell versus fixed-support geometry, periodic images, units, precision, and propagation kernel explicit. The JAX MD implementation demonstrates why a coordinate transformation and its custom derivative rule belong to the scientific contract. This is a reference for an audit, not a claim that its integrator reproduces the repository's kernel. **C.** [JAX MD space][jax-space]; [integrator][jax-simulate].

### 2.4 Two new 2026 precedents sharpen rollout validation

**Chen, Costa and Zavadlav, “SupraTITO,” v1, 1 October 2026. M.** The study extends finite-lag generation to periodic dipeptide assemblies. Its molecule-consistent displacement targets avoid separating bonded sites across periodic images; it distinguishes physical lag from flow time and compares contact-memory and structural time series. It explicitly limits contact persistence to its output resolution and avoids diffusion-coefficient claims without winding information. The architecture is not exactly rotation equivariant and conditions on sequence/topology, so it is not a geometry-only native replacement. Homogeneous dipeptides and a restricted training concentration/system size limit transfer claims. The useful contribution to E0/E2/S6 is a more careful target/periodicity and temporally resolved validation pattern, not its chemical inputs or training budget. [Methods, metrics, limitations][supratito].

**Tan, Li and Wang, “Generative Modeling of Stochastic Dynamics for Long-Time Evolution,” v1, 30 September 2026. M.** Conditional diffusion kernels are tested through long-time correlations and coarsening in Model B and through driven-colloid observables. The supplement makes composition conditional on a complete autonomous Markov state; colored-noise variables must be carried if they supply memory. It distinguishes exact-kernel composition from accumulated learned-kernel error and notes that sampled endpoints do not specify intervening paths. This supports the feasibility of short-lag kernels, not the sufficiency of this repository's local projected observations. **Transfer:** E2/E3/S4 should test the claimed observable dynamics and retained state; neither endpoint fit nor repeating a recurrence supplies that validation. Code was not available for this refresh. [Checked supplement and validation][tan].

## 3. Nucleation: physical interpretation, ascertainment, and trials

### 3.1 Preserve complementary structural evidence and contrary mechanisms

The established literature distinguishes local crystalline order, spatially correlated liquid order, persistent establishment, criticality, rates, and mechanistic influence. PTM and bond-order measurements motivate structural reference views; neither supplies a committor. Neighbor-averaged bond order observes a larger support than a purely local descriptor. A local entropy fingerprint is a useful template-independent geometry control, while adding its enthalpy companion would change the input contract. **Retained primary methods.** [Steinhardt et al.][steinhardt]; [Lechner–Dellago][lechner]; [PTM][ptm]; [entropy fingerprint][entropy]; [topological cluster classification][tcc].

| ten Wolde, Ruiz-Montero and Frenkel (1996), Lennard-Jones nucleation | Separate barrier sampling from dynamical crossing and rate estimation | A persistent-size catalogue is a different measurement; LJ thresholds are not Al thresholds. [Source][tenwolde] |
| Díaz Leines and Rogal (2018), Ni | Prestructured surroundings improve a reaction-coordinate description | A Ni path ensemble is not prospective Al birth prediction or proof that a wider learned context adds information. [Source][leines] |
| Liang et al. (2020), Ni3Al | Size alone can miss crystallinity and chemical-order distinctions | Alloy chemical order does not authorize species channels in the native encoder. [Source][liang] |

| Chapman et al. (2023), SODAS; Men (2024); Freitas–Reed (2020) | Continuous Al disorder and liquid-side interface structure have close precedents | Interface characterization/growth does not establish independent homogeneous birth lead time. [SODAS][sodas]; [Men][men]; [Freitas–Reed][freitas] |

| Tipeev–Zanotto (2025), Al | A recent liquid-trained potential and entropy-based cluster analysis provide a close comparison | Generating potential and kinetic protocol differ; numerical settings require a separate full-method audit. **A in the retained ledger.** [Source][tipeev] |

The first four September ledgers preserve fuller accounts and their reading limitations. The review should retain these counterexamples when an appealing learned color field suggests a precursor. “Not detected by PTM” does not mean physically unstructured, and “more ordered” does not universally mean more likely to establish.

**New 2025 addition — Deng et al., “The role of fluctuations in the nucleation process,” v2, 27 June 2025. M.** In a Lennard-Jones/argon-like system, committor-guided enhanced sampling finds nonspherical nuclei and an interface reported as more disordered than bulk liquid. This is a relevant counterweight to a universal ordered-shell mechanism. Its variational functional is Boltzmann-ensemble based, with mass-weighted derivatives and defined basins; it does not supply finite-horizon cooling-risk labels. **Transfer:** keep competing structural interpretations and a complete support definition in H4/H7/S6. A successful ordered-shell explanation in another material must be tested rather than built into eligibility or training labels. [Checked system and method][deng].

### 3.2 A committor, a useful sampling coordinate, and a rate are not the same validation

Jung et al. use learned fate predictions to guide path sampling and discover mechanisms; Lazzeri et al. reconstruct an approximate equilibrium path ensemble from biased path acquisition. These are close precedents for data-efficient shooting. They also show why physical propagation and how paths were sampled must be distinguished. Equilibrium reweighting cannot simply be imported into a selected cooling cohort or a changed bath. **A/C for Jung et al.; A for Lazzeri et al.; pinned shooting code below.** [Jung et al.][jung]; [Lazzeri et al.][lazzeri].

**New 2026 addition — Trizio, Rossi and Parrinello, “Ceci n'est pas un committor, yet it samples like one,” v2, 27 February. M.** Replacing coordinate-gradient terms with a descriptor-space criterion gives an approximate variational target and reduces cost. The authors explicitly separate efficient enhanced sampling from recovering the exact committor. Their iterative procedure also reweights configurations using its known bias. The relevant lesson for H2/H5/E8 is to declare whether the deliverable is an interventional derivative, calibrated hitting probability, or useful acquisition coordinate. One can succeed without establishing the others. The derivative shortcut is not a repair for an incompatible native observation, and its Boltzmann/bias weights cannot be invented for historical adaptive records. [Checked reformulation and workflow][trizio].

**New lead, abstract only — Naguszewski and Quigley, “Learned Committors as Reaction Coordinates for Nucleation Rates,” v1, 9 July 2026. A.** The indexed primary abstract reports an Ising example where a geometric cluster coordinate recovers rates despite weak pointwise committor prediction. This further motivates distinguishing aggregate-rate and individual-state validation. Full-method retrieval failed in this refresh; no estimator or claimed general theorem is imported. It is a reading lead for a future rate-specific task, not an additional E8 experiment. [Primary abstract][naguszewski].

**Updated publication record — Montero de Hijes, Falkner and Dellago.** The ice-nucleation study inspected in Phase 1 as arXiv:2512.01479v1 is now published as JCP 164, 094501 (2026). Its mW recurrence analysis motivates checking whether a projected nucleus coordinate retains history, even after approximate committor learning. That does not make the underlying full physical dynamics non-Markovian, establish the same effect in Al, or authorize age inputs. **M for the previously inspected preprint; publication metadata verified.** [Preprint][ice]; [published DOI][ice-doi]. This informs H3/S4's interpretation; recurrence fields can remain diagnostic metadata.

### 3.3 Competing outcomes and the population being estimated

The mstate primary software paper provides established transition-specific competing-risk/multistate tools. It is useful for separating establishment, physical dissolution/coalescence, and administrative censoring. It does not turn tracking failure into a physical event or validate conditional independent censoring. **A for the software paper; reference documentation was also checked.** [de Wreede, Fiocco and Putter][mstate].

For H4, common complete-case eligibility can differ from short-horizon ascertainment. The repository's positive-confirmation exemption and delayed confirmation clock must be preserved. An onset-by-h target is different from confirmation-by-h; inverse-censoring weights cannot recover deterministically excluded records or justify unknown labels as negatives. The first evidence needed is a valid present-eligible denominator and known/unknown outcome mass. A competing-risk neural model is conditional work after that ledger, not a substitute for it. [Cohort producer][embryo-code]; [metric contract][embryo-metric].

For H5/H6, repeated archive views, independent shots, different velocities, different hidden exteriors, and different kernels are distinct objects. A coordinate hash does not identify a complete dynamical start. More shots improve conditional information but should not silently increase a configuration's scientific population weight. Hoffman, Sen and Weinberg's work on informative cluster size supports specifying which marginal population is being estimated; it does not prescribe one universally correct hierarchy. **A.** [Within-cluster resampling][hoffman].

These ideas were already present in the repository's nucleation cautions. The new contribution is the concrete producer/weight bridge in E1, followed by compatible prediction reuse. H4–H6 do not automatically require new MD. E8 is justified only when retained state/trial information cannot resolve a specific surviving question, with attempted and unresolved trials kept visible.

## 4. Experiment selection: compare procedures and close explanations

Cawley and Talbot treat selection as part of the learning procedure: optimizing a noisy criterion can distort the final comparison. This supports separating fitting, selection, calibration, and final assessment, and measuring variation in the fitted readout. It does not establish that any particular repository ranking is biased or invalidate an ancestor bootstrap. **M.** [Primary article][selection]. Huan and Marzouk formalize simulation-based Bayesian experimental design. Here only the limited principle transfers: prefer a comparison whose possible outcomes distinguish live explanations. No expected information gain, Bayesian utility, or compute saving has been calculated. **A.** [Primary abstract][design].

**S5 is a procedure question, not another encoder leaderboard.** The repository's readout fixes initialization and sampling through a study seed, fits source-weighted feature scaling, selects by NLL, and calibrates in a distinct role. Changing head seed can change both initialization and sampled optimization history. E7 must therefore fix sampling mode, update budget, candidate selection schedule, scaling, and calibration, then report that procedure's variation separately from encoder seeds and source uncertainty. [Readout producer][onset-eval].

The planned frozen-readout comparison answers whether retained maturity rankings depend on that procedure. It does not identify a maturity-by-adaptation interaction: a flat frozen comparison can coexist with different fine-tuning responses. The latter remains a distinct conditional E9 experiment. Likewise, a small non-significant difference does not establish equivalence without a justified practical margin. If existing held-out sources have already informed the research sequence, subsequent comparisons on them remain exploratory; a genuinely unused eligible cohort is required for later prospective confirmation. A new file name does not restore independence.

**The cross-study process finding is narrower than “poor reproducibility.”** The project already maintains substantial result receipts, uncertainty, and failed branches. September's forecasting protocol explicitly included trained anchor/mean controls, repeated/reversed past, change/increment checks, and whole-path scores. The present Ni evaluator's blockwise endpoint scores answer a narrower question. The missing inheritance is claim-specific validation across branches, not a missing generic tracking system. [Earlier protocol][old-forecast]; [Ni evaluator][ni-eval]; [results system][results-system].

The practical update is to carry the relevant evidence obligation with each claim. A simulator claim inherits emitted-path and temporal-sensitivity checks; an establishment-risk claim inherits ascertainment and population accounting; a structural-retention claim inherits matched domains and readouts. Preserve each historical metric contract and add a named analysis when its target changes. Close an explanation only to the precision supported by its contrast, recording what a null rules out and what remains unresolved. Expensive architecture comparisons are deferred when a producer, denominator, metric, or target mismatch can still explain the apparent deficit.
