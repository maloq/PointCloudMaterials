AP is source-weighted average precision for sustained local onset within the
stated 3/6/12 ps horizon, with 3 ps primary.
Higher AP and lower Brier are better. These are the jointly trained heads with
checkpoint selection by tuning AP subject to physical-retention constraints.

At the secondary 12 ps horizon, C has the highest joint AP point estimate, about 49% above B. Its paired
whole-root bootstrap AP difference versus B has a 95% interval of
[-0.0094, 0.1204], so the improvement is uncertain. H versus B is also uncertain:
[-0.0279, 0.1511]. D versus B has interval [0.0020, 0.0709], but that comparison
adds both ranking and a teacher; it does not isolate the teacher's effect.
Adding a teacher to C lowered the 12 ps AP point estimate. Tensor pooling and
noise training did not improve the 12 ps AP point estimate in this budget. At 3 ps,
D and E are close to C (about 0.26), far below the relaxed-input point estimate.

Fresh frozen linear/MLP probes use tuning NLL selection, not the joint head's AP
selection. Their results test information accessibility under a different
readout procedure and must remain separate. C's MLP-probe AP12 is 0.1432. Many MLP
probes selected the permitted constant-risk step 0 checkpoint, including H, and
therefore have AP12 0.0325 (AP3 0.0050). A higher-capacity readout was not automatically better.

At 12 ps, the matched relaxed-descriptor MLP achieved AP 0.1461 and Brier 0.03092. H has a
higher AP point estimate but slightly worse Brier; risk calibration remains a
separate requirement. At the threshold selected for 5% tuning false positives,
C's development recall/FPR are 40.4%/8.47%, and H's are 55.4%/5.81%. These must not
be described as recalls measured at exactly 5% development FPR.

Arithmetic means of two matched seeds at the predeclared final 24-epoch
checkpoint. Static structural metrics first average the same three Al frames.
These encoders use unsupervised temporal alignment and the indicated
regularization; their prediction assay fits a separate hazard conditioned on
current physical descriptors. This is a different training/readout protocol from
the onset-oriented joint-head comparison above.

At 3 ps, VICReg has the best final AP of these three objectives.
At 12 ps, Epi + variance AP is 0.1452/0.1341 in the two seeds, versus VICReg's 0.1097/0.1327
and plain Epi's 0.0736/0.0775. These point estimates favor the variance-floor
variant for onset within this study; two seeds do not establish robustness.

At 12 ps, the existing current-physics hazard has Brier 0.03156. All final encoders have
worse Brier point estimates. Epi + variance's paired Brier differences have
source intervals [-0.00484, 0.00701] and [-0.00461, 0.00711]; both include zero.
Its 9 ps residual-order MSE is 1.33263, versus the zero-residual baseline 1.33202.
Consequently, the study does not establish additional calibrated predictive
information beyond current physics. Plain Epi's better structural decoding and
higher liquid rank do not translate into better onset prediction.

Dataset rank is the source-weighted centered covariance participation ratio on
all 2880 paired observations, including fit/tuning/development. Movement rank
uses uncentered increments on the dense observed-trajectory chart. These are
linear effective dimensions, not nonlinear intrinsic-manifold estimates.
Movement d95 counts principal directions accounting for 95% of increment energy.
The Epi table's liquid rank uses a different population and is not interchangeable.

C improved onset AP without appreciably changing normalized temporal movement
or added-noise response relative to B. Tensor variants increased spectral
dimensions while worsening AP and these sensitivity diagnostics. None reaches
a normalized 0.75 ps jump RMS of 0.10 under this implementation. H's dense chart
uses observed coordinates, so its temporal scores are a domain-transfer check,
not a trajectory of individually relaxed inputs. Noise and jump responses use
different clean reference populations; their magnitudes are not a direct ratio
of physical motion to measurement noise.

## What these screens establish

The forecast development cohort contains 15 reused roots and 643 eligible windows.
It has 3/7/18 positive windows at 3/6/12 ps respectively; source-weighted prevalence
is 0.50%/1.24%/3.25%.
The robust/context studies use one seed each. Bootstrap intervals measure source
uncertainty conditional on the fitted models, not seed uncertainty, multiplicity
across model searches or unseen-test generalization. Static structure is a
transductive screen; dense dynamics are descriptive and can overlap encoder
fitting roots. The separate readout protocols prevent a universal cross-study
AP leaderboard.

Retain C and H as onset finalists, with late spatial-context fusion as a useful
addition to test. Confirm them with matched readouts and new ancestry-disjoint
evaluation before another large parameter search. Keep plain Epi as evidence
that structural retention and onset skill differ; Epi + variance is its more
promising12 ps variant but is worse than VICReg at 3 ps, with no demonstrated
calibrated benefit beyond
the physical baseline. No new training was launched for this report.
