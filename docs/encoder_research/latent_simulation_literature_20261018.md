# Recent methods for the Ni latent simulator

**Primary-source review: 18 October 2026.** This follow-up selects methods against the completed October 15 Ni experiments. It supplements the [October 11 literature review](literature.md), whose historical reading labels and conclusions remain intact. This is a research shortlist and proposed comparison, not an implemented or submitted queue.

The closest architectural precedent is **LaM-SLidE**. The most useful complementary directions are **FMRC** for learning representations through conditional future distributions, **PFCG** for explicit memory, **STAR-MD** for robustness to imperfect history, and **SelfTICA** for optimizing temporal representations. **SupraTITO** is a precedent for the later, spatially coupled simulator needed to generate collective assembly.

Relevant primary methods and results were read, with publication records and linked repositories checked where available. No external model was reproduced. Only the PFCG training-window implementation was specifically inspected here; a repository link elsewhere establishes availability, not implementation verification. Dates distinguish publication from preprint revision. The search emphasized 2025–2026, retaining older papers when unusually close to our task; it is a targeted review, not an exhaustive systematic survey.

## What our experiments require

The current observation is a fixed central atom followed over time. Its 244 measurements comprise bond order, TDA, density and velocity summaries. Regional experiments add 87 context measurements. Four observations at native 0.1 ps spacing span **0.3 ps**; the forecast covers 60 subsequent observations, or **6 ps after the final observed frame**. A 32-dimensional representation and a conditional mixture predictor generate independent local paths.

Three completed findings motivate distinct changes:

- **Some useful structural information survives compression.** In locally liquid test states, linear VAMP retains qbar6/coherence/wbar6 with within-source R² of 0.827/0.929/0.885; nonlinear VAMP gives 0.381/0.330/0.017. These are reconstruction diagnostics, not proof of a productive precursor. [Local experiment](../../experiments/ni_kinetic_latent_20261015/README.md#liquid-order-results-october-15).
- **Generated dynamics remain wrong even with useful representations.** Observed high-order occupancy is 14.8%; generated occupancy is 21.8% for linear VAMP and 28.8% for VAE. The strict liquid forecast panel has only one actual local crystalline entry, so it cannot validate nucleation kinetics. [Same experiment](../../experiments/ni_kinetic_latent_20261015/README.md#liquid-order-results-october-15).
- **Spatial information helps detection without solving generation.** At 6 ps, 20 Å context improves default-nucleus latent AP from 0.675 to 0.770, but direct measurements already give 0.772. Long-path scores do not improve correspondingly, and raw decoding has numerical and physical failures. [Spatial comparison](../../experiments/ni_spatial_scales_20261015/README.md#completed-comparison-october-15).

Thus representation quality, missing memory, output distribution, decoded validity and spatial coupling should be varied separately. Adding larger attention layers alone would not identify which problem was addressed.

## Closest methods

### 1. LaM-SLidE: generate an entire latent trajectory

**NeurIPS 2025; latest preprint revision January 2026.** Cross-attention compresses identifiable entities into latent tokens. A temporal generative model produces future latent sequences; a frozen decoder retrieves entity properties. Molecular experiments additionally differentiate decoded geometry losses through that decoder. Evaluations include MD17 and peptide structural/dynamical distribution comparisons. This supports whole-segment latent generation, but does not guarantee arbitrary-duration stability or correct nucleation rates. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/cd04ec5aebfbe397c7fd718c35d02e0b-Paper-Conference.pdf) · [latest methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12128v5) · [code](https://github.com/ml-jku/LaM-SLidE).

**Our adaptation:** first reuse the existing linear-VAMP and VAE representations. Generate the 60-state future block jointly, with attention across physical observation positions, and score decoded measurement paths. Compare with generation directly in the 244 measured dimensions to expose compression costs. A frozen decoder still supplies gradients to the trajectory generator. This proposal does not restart discontinued physical-reconstruction encoder pretraining. Entity identifiers become useful if we later evolve many centers together.

### 2. FMRC: learn a representation through conditional distributions

**Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation, January 2025 issue; online December 2024.** Flow Matching for Reaction Coordinates jointly trains a compressed representation and forward/backward conditional flow models on lagged observations. It aims to retain transition-distribution information; protein experiments evaluate reaction coordinates and derived Markov state models. Its theoretical treatment assumes reversible dynamics, which we cannot presume for the selected Ni growth histories. It is principally a representation method, rather than a demonstrated unrestricted trajectory simulator. [Methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.17139v2) · [publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39699247/) · [code](https://github.com/Mingyuan00/Flow_Matching_for_RC).

**Our adaptation:** condition a future-measurement distribution on a 32-dimensional encoding of the observed history. This directly addresses the original question of whether nearby representations imply similar possible futures. A forward-only history-conditioned version would be an adaptation, not full FMRC, and would not inherit its reversible-process claims. Compare it against a fixed representation with the same conditional generator.

### 3. PFCG: explicit memory of resolved motion

**Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation, online April 2026.** Probabilistic Forecasting for Coarse-Grained Molecular Dynamics combines a current-state network with GRUs reading preceding coordinate increments and predicts a Gaussian displacement. It reports improved molecular first-passage behavior for several processes. Its five adjacent training terms use observed sliding windows, not recursively generated inputs; this was checked in the released implementation. Its histories generally span much longer physical intervals than ours. [Paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13157819/) · [code](https://gitlab.com/pak-group/pfcg_m01) · [inspected implementation](https://gitlab.com/pak-group/pfcg_m01/-/raw/main/PFCG.py).

**Our adaptation:** keep present measurements as an anchor and give a separate branch their actual preceding increments. Compare current-state and recurrent-memory models with the same output distribution. More observations mean more physical history only if genuinely earlier frames are retrieved; moving the forecast origin changes the task. Do not copy the paper's random-window splitting or validation selection.

### 4. STAR-MD: make prediction robust to imperfect history

**ICLR 2026.** STAR-MD uses causal temporal attention and diffusion to generate protein conformations. Small diffusion corruption of historical context during training and inference improves long-rollout robustness. Its temporal evaluations include autocorrelation and lagged structural comparisons. Protein-specific state representations and much coarser physical strides differ from our Ni setting; structurally valid samples are not sufficient evidence of accurate event probabilities. [Paper and publication status](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02128) · [methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.02128v2).

**Our adaptation:** add a declared context-corruption treatment to an otherwise unchanged predictor. Cross it separately with the choice of Gaussian-mixture versus flow output. Test both raw validity and temporal distributions, counting every failed rollout. No dedicated STAR-MD code release was verified; the related ConfRover repository must not be presented as that implementation. Do not import explicit physical-time or temperature covariates into our model.

### 5. SelfTICA: a different way to optimize temporal features

**June 2026 preprint, revised September.** SelfTICA uses a contrastive temporal objective and a predictor, avoiding gradients through spectral decompositions. With an optimal linear predictor, the objective connects to VAMP-2. Its graph construction distinguishes persistent focal-system nodes from surrounding environment nodes. Molecular and surface-reaction demonstrations support representation learning; the paper does not establish autonomous generative stability. [Methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.15495v3) · [code and examples](https://github.com/Kai-Zhu-2001/SelfTICA) · [mlcolvar tutorial](https://mlcolvar.readthedocs.io/en/release-2.0/notebooks/tutorials/cvs_SelfTICA.html).

**Our adaptation:** retain 244/331 measured inputs, 32 latent dimensions and a common downstream generator. This isolates whether optimization helps relative to our weak nonlinear VAMP result. A later focal-region/environment graph is a separate coordinate-input experiment: the existing 87 aggregate context values do not provide graph connectivity. Precise equilibrium or slow-mode interpretations still require an appropriate ensemble.

### 6. SupraTITO: jointly evolve a periodic interacting system

**October 2026 preprint; already present in the October 11 review.** SupraTITO learns finite-lag conditional flows of periodic, molecule-consistent displacements for coarse-grained peptide assembly. It evolves interacting entities jointly and tests collective assembly statistics. This is a useful spatial precedent, but its molecular representation, training population and physical conditions differ from atomistic Ni. A public implementation was not verified. [Methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01381v1).

**Our adaptation:** eventually evolve interacting center tokens with periodic relative geometry and shared noise/coupling, or generate joint atomic states. Independent local predictions cannot demonstrate a connected nucleus. A latent field would still need a consistent connectivity/geometry definition before claiming generated 32- or 64-atom nuclei; feeding outer measurements into a single-center predictor is insufficient.

## Specific relevance to pre-nucleation

**Persistent structure needs its own diagnostic.** The 2025 hidden-information study finds that subtle ice/water environments can occupy very low-variance descriptor directions and become harder to recover under particular feature combinations. Onion clustering analyzes persistent environments across temporal windows, while LeaPP groups individual atomic paths through crystallization. These support testing whether latent trajectories retain fleeting versus persistent ordering, rather than interpreting a visually separated cluster as a precursor. LeaPP uses full paths and subsequent nucleus participation: it is retrospective, and time warping cannot validate physical event timing. [Hidden-information study](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.jctc.5c00374) · [Onion clustering](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2403771121) · [Dynsight code](https://github.com/GMPavanLab/dynsight) · [LeaPP](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.jctc.5c00097).

**A good reaction coordinate need not eliminate memory.** A 2026 ice-nucleation study tests recurrence statistics and finds history dependence even after improving the reaction coordinate using committor information. This directly motivates checking whether preceding observations add information conditional on our present latent state. It does not prove the same mechanism in Ni. [Published paper](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0314412) · [accessible manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01479).

**Reactive-path discovery is a separate use of a generator.** GenAIMMD proposes shooting configurations using a learned generator and then runs physical trajectories. Gen-COMPAS combines generated intermediate proposals, committor information and physical path construction. Both are relevant to acquiring informative continuations, but reactive sampling does not directly yield unconditional birth probabilities or natural rates. Our local measurements cannot directly provide valid whole-cell configurations or the physical weights required by those workflows. [GenAIMMD, September 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20461v1) · [code](https://github.com/CompPhysVienna/paper_genaimmd) · [Gen-COMPAS, Nature, September 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11025-1) · [code](https://github.com/Tangcyu/Gen-COMPAS).

## Proposed comparison order

These are our adaptations and hypotheses, not performance claims from the papers.

1. **Change the propagator while preserving useful representations.** Fixed linear VAMP and VAE; existing mixture predictor versus joint latent flow generation, plus a direct-measurement generator control. Keep four observed frames and the same 60-frame future. Frozen decoded-measurement supervision addresses what the generated latent trajectory means physically; it must not silently replace a distribution objective with stochastic-sample averaging to a mean.
2. **Isolate memory and robustness.** Current-state, GRU-history and exponential-filter-history arms sharing an output head; separately test context corruption. Preserve origins and exact cadence. Longer histories require actual earlier observations and a common availability panel for that comparison. Filters require declared initialization or observed burn-in.
3. **Change the representation with the propagator fixed.** SelfTICA versus existing linear VAMP/nonlinear VAMP/VAE. Separately test FMRC-inspired joint conditional-distribution training, explicitly marking its changed objective and assumptions. Keep local and 20 Å context comparisons visible.
4. **Evaluate a collective spatial model only after local trajectory quality improves.** Couple many centers from the same physical source state. Establish consistency between neighboring outputs and a physical connectivity definition before claiming simulated nucleation.

Use only train/test with preserved ancestor roles; fit transforms on train and freeze budgets, objectives and checkpoint rules before test evaluation. The existing test sources have already informed research decisions, so further results are development evidence. No AP-based training or selection, added neighborhood exclusion, temperature/age covariates, or broad spatial replay belongs in these comparisons.

For every generator, report decoded validity, marginal and connected-path scores, increments, autocorrelation, order occupancy/dwell times, and event probabilities. Retain invalid paths in the accounting. Compare generated local-order entry with actual MD, separately from the established full-nucleus assay. Investigate candidate precursors using declared event-aligned, present-order and regional-phase diagnostics without changing catalogue membership. The existing one-entry strict-liquid panel is too small to settle productive generation.

**Time and simulator semantics:** flow/denoising time transforms random noise into a sampled future; it is not physical MD time. Training on 0.1 ps observations does not validate femtosecond intermediate motion. A finite-lag generator should first reproduce the native observed path law. Short NVE derivative teachers remain a different protocol from the saved NPT histories and should not be assumed to enforce their longer-time dynamics.
