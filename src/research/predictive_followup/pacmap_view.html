How to read these maps
  • Each encoder retains its recorded weights. Its exported coordinates are standardized using source-weighted training means and standard deviations. PaCMAP uses 4,224 training observations; 2,285 held-out observations are added through its out-of-sample transform. Validation and test observations do not fit the scaler, PCA or training layout.
  • PaCMAP 0.9.1: 10 neighbors, mid-near ratio 0.5, far-pair ratio 2, Euclidean distance, PCA initialization and the library's training-fitted PCA preprocessing to 100 dimensions when the input has more than 100 dimensions, 450 iterations, seed 20261001. Each map is independent: axes, rotation and separation are not directly comparable across panels.
  • Future colors are measured from 12 shooting branches, never encoder inputs or projection-fitting labels. Crystalline fraction measures the local future neighborhood within 8 Å among its nearest 80 atoms; it is not a crystallization-event probability. Clear liquid means a noncrystalline center more than 8 Å from any present crystalline atom.
  • Every point has equal visual size. Sampling is stratified by present structure, so point density does not represent population prevalence. The same color range is used across panels for the selected population; filtering keeps the full map's axis extent.
  • Default new encoder: full targets, unconstrained variance, first declared training seed 20261001, selected by validation moment likelihood. All four treatments and all three seeds are available; no seed was chosen for appearance. Frozen encoders do not change with that selector.
  • MM-TDA has a larger 256-dimensional embedding and broader historical pretraining than the 128-dimensional references. These visualizations do not establish predictive sufficiency or rank encoder quality. Six held-out sources are historical shooting sources, not the Al64 window benchmark.
