__TITLE__

Frozen encoders; no neural training. These are independent 2D and 3D PaCMAP projections of the same atoms. Cluster colors come from the original high-dimensional spaces. Rotate and zoom the 3D view; hover for atom identity. Separate layouts have arbitrary axes and orientation.

Physical region codes: 0 boundary atom, 1 nearby crystalline center, 2 nearby connected disorder, 3 nearby small disordered pocket, 4 farther than 12 Å, 5 no qualifying interface. PTM codes: 0 unclassified, 1 FCC, 2 HCP, 3 BCC; other codes retain their producer values. Interface distance is negative on the crystalline side and positive on the disordered side, measured to the nearest crystal-side boundary atom; it is not an exact continuum surface. Missing interfaces are gray. Independently fitted cluster IDs do not share a physical identity. Filtering retains the existing layout; it does not refit PaCMAP.
