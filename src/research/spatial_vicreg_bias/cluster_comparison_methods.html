Methods

Neural colors maximize shared membership with descriptor clusters using a one-to-one assignment on all 684,723 grid centers. The mapping stays fixed across snapshots and frame/source filters for each model/descriptor pair. Labels N and D retain the original neural and descriptor IDs. Shared colors indicate an assigned pair; the matrix and intersection/union bars show its actual overlap.

Correspondence statistics use the currently displayed PaCMAP centers. Static frame sliders synchronize PaCMAP and MD; the MD z filter is independent. Highlight interface layers emphasizes points within 12 Å of the physical interface without changing the displayed population or correspondence statistics. All frames restores pooled PaCMAP. Axes are independent projections. Neural coordinates, clusters and models are unchanged. Descriptor clusters use all training environments. MD outlines show nonperiodic coordinate bounds. Local examples are uniform samples from the full selected snapshot, not fitted centroids; each has 80 atoms and an original center ID. Metric definitions.
