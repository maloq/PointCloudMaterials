## Matched early evidence: alignment trades structure for prediction

Means over three seeds at epoch4. Structural values average the saved Al static
frames within each seed; predictive values use the unchanged all64 source-held-out
population and the frozen128-unit nonlinear readout. Lower liquid-neighbor NMSE
and predictive log loss are better; higher nonbulk accuracy and spatial AUC are
better. Static frames are exploratory because training ancestry is not fully
established, and all are relaxed inputs; predictive evaluation uses observed inputs.

| Encoder | Liquid-neighbor NMSE ↓ | Nonbulk balanced accuracy ↑ | Nonbulk spatial AUC ↑ | Raw6-ps log loss ↓ | Calibrated6-ps log loss ↓ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Shared initialization, epoch0 (approximately) | 1.4815 | 0.642 | 0.5748 | 0.07165 | 0.07121 |
| R0: Epi+variance, no alignment | 1.3022 | 0.6622 | 0.5774 | 0.07759 | 0.07444 |
| R1: Epi+variance, aligned | 1.4002 | 0.6568 | 0.5386 | 0.07005 | 0.06942 |
| R2: VICReg | 1.5228 | 0.6621 | 0.5206 | 0.07089 | 0.07000 |

R0 has lower liquid-neighbor error than R1 in every seed, but worse predictive
log loss in every seed. R1 improves liquid-neighbor error against VICReg in all
three seeds; its calibrated predictive score is better in two of three seeds,
with a modest mean difference. Nonbulk balanced accuracy does not follow the
liquid-neighbor ranking. Spatial boundary discrimination declines under aligned
training. A single clustering, smoothness or readout score cannot certify the
representation as good for every purpose.

VICReg epoch8 has mean calibrated6-ps log loss0.06925 and liquid-neighbor
NMSE1.5116. R0/R1 epoch8 evaluations are missing: comparing that value with their
epoch4 results would confound recipe and training duration. We cannot yet tell
whether the structural/predictive trade-off persists at convergence.

## Completed readout controls: descriptors add accessible information

These are the previously frozen checkpoint controls, not the new three-seed
epoch24 endpoints. All entries below use calibrated6-ps test log loss. `d32`
contains same-patch radial/count/angular descriptors. `P32(z)` is a fixed
train-only projection that adds32 redundant features. Thus joint and redundant
inputs have identical dimension and predictor parameter counts.

| Historical observed-input export | z128, head128 | z128+d32, head128 | z128+P32(z), head128 | z128, head256 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Exact retained Epi initialization | 0.07044 | 0.06909 | 0.07118 | 0.07045 |
| Epi pretrained | 0.07073 | 0.06793 | 0.07017 | 0.07022 |
| Epi likelihood-adapted | 0.06916 | 0.06743 | 0.06933 | 0.06898 |
| VICReg pretrained | 0.06989 | 0.06903 | 0.07157 | 0.07114 |
| VICReg likelihood-adapted | 0.06991 | 0.06833 | 0.07061 | 0.06992 |
| Scratch likelihood-trained | 0.07010 | 0.06936 | 0.07026 | 0.06993 |

For pretrained Epi, descriptor add-back reduces log loss by0.002796 against z
(paired source-bootstrap95% interval[-0.003745,-0.002017]) and by0.002239 against
the dimension-matched redundant control ([-0.003202,-0.001320]). For adapted Epi,
the corresponding differences are-0.001734 ([-0.002491,-0.001061]) and-0.001898
([-0.002970,-0.001057]). These intervals condition on the fitted encoder and
readout; they do not quantify training-seed uncertainty or correct for all
comparisons. The scratch add-back contrast against z is uncertain.

Doubling readout width is not a consistent repair. The add-back benefit persists
for pretrained Epi/VICReg with head256, whereas several adapted-model contrasts
become uncertain. A random retained encoder also supports a useful nonlinear
readout. This supports distinguishing **information accessibility** from apparent
information loss; finite probes cannot prove that information is intrinsically
absent from an embedding.

Train-standardized/PCA32 distances change the kNN forecasts little for the
trained exports. For adapted Epi, calibrated log loss is0.07128 with raw distance,
0.07084 standardized, and0.07081 with PCA32, versus0.06916 with the nonlinear
readout. For the retained initialization, standardization improves0.07718 to
0.07618, still above0.07044 from the nonlinear readout. This tested distance
rescaling does not resolve the readout gap.

Same-support descriptor-only controls are weaker here: density26/head128 gives
0.08212; SOAP50/head128 gives0.08907. These specific implementations and budgets
do not establish a universal ranking of learned features versus SOAP.

## Matched displacement is not yet evidence of useful invariance

At epoch4, mean source real-MD/synthetic response ratios are approximately0.102
(R0),0.087(R1),0.101(R2), compared with0.207 at initialization. The control matches
per-pair displacement RMS across the same tracked79 noncentral atoms. All
trained arms respond much more strongly to the independent Gaussian displacement
than to real MD. R1 suppresses both real and synthetic responses relative to R0;
these results do not establish selective preservation of meaningful rearrangement.
Gaussian displacements are not a thermal MD distribution and may produce
unphysical close contacts. The ratio alone must not be treated as an encoder
quality objective or causal signal-to-noise measure.

## Birth coverage: this held-out sample cannot validate precursors

The audit processed all15 selection,15 calibration and30 test sources. It found
34/41/67 candidate birth events respectively, but candidate-centered examples
are not the outcome-independent natural-risk population.

| Natural-risk cohort, current observation only | Selection | Calibration | Test |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Eligible observations, established-crystal-clear8Å | 22,884 | 18,152 | 47,357 |
| Birth-positive observations within3ps | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Birth-positive observations within6ps | 0 | 2 | 1 |
| Eligible observations, no PTM crystal within8Å | 19,934 | 15,701 | 41,840 |
| Birth-positive observations within6ps, no PTM crystal8Å | 0 | 0 | 0 |

The single6-ps test positive is one distinct birth event in one source. The
established-clear8Å test cohort also has1,123 competing contact-positive
observations within6ps. At32Å, no-PTM-crystal eligibility shrinks to140/64/415
observations and still zero births. This is insufficient coverage, not evidence
that precursors do not exist. Existing atom-onset performance must not be
presented as demonstrated nucleus-birth prediction. No birth predictor is fitted
from this unsupported cohort.
