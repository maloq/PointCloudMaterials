# Encoder mechanisms: results after the first evaluation recovery

28 September 2026. All nine encoders completed 24 training epochs. Combining
the original and first recovered results gives **50/54 quality evaluations and
49/54 displacement evaluations**. Seven of nine evaluation workers completed;
two stopped during plotting. The nine supervised adaptation fits have not yet
started. These counts describe the cutoff before the second recovery below.

## Complete matched contrast: alignment at 24 epochs

Both Epi arms have all three seeds evaluated at the fixed epoch-24 endpoint.
Values are means across seeds; structural values first average the three Al
static frames within seed. Predictive scores use the fixed all64 test sources,
observed geometry and the same frozen 128-unit nonlinear readout. Lower NMSE
and log loss are better; higher balanced accuracy/spatial AUC are better.

| Treatment | Liquid-neighbor NMSE | Nonbulk balanced accuracy | Nonbulk spatial AUC | Raw 6-ps log loss | Calibrated 6-ps log loss |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Epi+variance, no alignment (R0) | 1.31270 | 0.65801 | 0.58122 | 0.07811 | 0.07496 |
| Epi+variance, aligned (R1) | 1.39056 | 0.65887 | 0.53880 | 0.06967 | 0.06843 |

Removing alignment improves liquid-neighbor error and boundary discrimination
but worsens predictive log loss in **every seed**. This trade-off remains after
24 epochs and is not explained solely by reading the encoders too early.
Nonbulk balanced accuracy is almost unchanged, so it would miss much of the
effect seen in physical-neighbor and boundary metrics.

| Seed | R0 liquid-neighbor NMSE | R1 liquid-neighbor NMSE | R0 calibrated 6-ps log loss | R1 calibrated 6-ps log loss |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 20260926 | 1.30133 | 1.39440 | 0.07506 | 0.06853 |
| 20260927 | 1.34308 | 1.43310 | 0.07473 | 0.06842 |
| 20260928 | 1.29370 | 1.34419 | 0.07510 | 0.06833 |

These are three-seed contrasts, not an equivalence claim or a newly selected
hyperparameter optimum. Static snapshots are relaxed inputs with incompletely
established training ancestry; the predictive assay uses held-out Al sources.
The tasks and domains must remain explicit when interpreting the trade-off.

## Training trajectory

| 0 | 1.48151 | 1.48151 | 0.07121 | 0.07121 |

| 12 | 1.31720 | 1.39414 | 0.07447 | 0.06928 |
| 18 | 1.31417 | 1.38754 | 0.07493 | 0.06879 |
| 24 | 1.31270 | 1.39056 | 0.07496 | 0.06843 |

All rows above have three seeds per arm. The initial structural change happens
mostly before epoch 4; liquid-neighbor error is then fairly stable. Aligned Epi's
predictive score continues improving through the fixed endpoint. Thus this
particular same-time observed/relaxed Epi experiment does **not** show continuing
erosion of liquid-neighbor structure from epoch 4 to 24. It does show that
alignment establishes a structural/predictive trade-off early. This does not
resolve historical GeoFormer or temporal-view experiments with different losses.

## VICReg is still missing one seed

At epoch 24, seeds 20260926/20260927 have liquid-neighbor NMSE 1.58247/1.51192
and calibrated 6-ps log loss 0.06832/0.06789. On those same seeds, aligned Epi
has NMSE 1.39440/1.43310 and log loss 0.06853/0.06842. VICReg is slightly better
predictively in these two seeds, while aligned Epi retains more liquid structure.
The third VICReg seed has no completed epoch-24 predictive evaluation yet;
do not compare its two-seed mean as if it were a complete three-seed result.

The earlier epoch-4 point-estimate advantage for Epi therefore does not establish
a lasting predictive advantage over VICReg. The final three-seed comparison and
the matched adaptation fits remain necessary.

## Recovery status

The original compilation-limit failure is resolved by process isolation. The
two new failures occur when the renderer independently refits K-means and then
requires exact agreement with the clustering already used for metrics. Both
failures happened in a Zr visualization. A fixed seed does not ensure identical
local optima across different numerical thread contexts.

The repaired renderer reuses the actual metric-fit estimator, saves its centers,
and retains the anchor-label equality check. On the cached failing Zr frame,
all 4,096 anchor labels agree with the retained estimator. Clustering settings,
training data, metric definitions and completed exports are preserved.

Recovery v3 reuses all 50 completed quality and 49 displacement assays by their
saved provenance and only fills the four/five missing assays. Slurm array
**1012979** is running on scheduler-selected RTX6000PRO nodes with node58
excluded; **1012980** queues the original nine adaptation fits afterward.
The previous permanently blocked dependency job was cancelled. See
[execution records](../../docs/encoder_mechanisms.md).

The completed readout controls and birth-coverage conclusions are unchanged;
see the [earlier detailed audit](RESULTS-20260928.md). Neither this onset assay
nor the current birth sample establishes precursor detection in Al, Ta or Zr.
