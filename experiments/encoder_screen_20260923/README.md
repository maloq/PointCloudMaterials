# Which retained snapshot encoders preserve liquid and interfacial structure?

The 35-pass GeoFrame reproduction separates two phenomena: encoder liquid-order
readout improves while projector readout declines, and better Al fault decoding
coexists with poorly separated liquid clusters. The original epoch34 VICReg and
epoch159 VISReg pictures were not a fixed-objective duration comparison.

This screen evaluates a broad, explicitly chosen queue before deciding what to
train next. It includes the two-seed MACE distance/future factorial, repaired
geometry/teacher controls, nine relaxed-input treatments, JEPA regularizer,
projector, direct-export, order-supervision and initialization controls, the
retained local GATr/broad MACE checkpoints, and historical VISReg endpoints.
Tensor-identical weights are removed; short runs and step0 controls retain their
actual budgets. The37 completed VICReg trajectory evaluations are reused.
No new training is claimed by evaluating more exports.

All snapshot models see fixed atom centers and the same independently computed
physical labels. Native input radii/caps/scales/precision remain model-specific;
Al-only models do not acquire an invented Ta/Zr species interpretation. Forecast
labels/splits/readout conditions are matched, and we require calibrated-error
and paired-root evidence alongside AP. History, velocity and larger computational
halo models require a distinct input protocol and are explicitly excluded.

Primary panels: within-liquid continuous-order/topology fidelity beyond density;
Al planar-fault and mixed-boundary AP with class counts; unsupervised K7 context
contingencies and nonbulk AMI; distance-matched local coherence against collapsed/
shuffled controls; rank and small-displacement response; 9 ps conditional future
residual error and 12 ps hazard Brier/NLL/AP, selected-step0 status and root intervals.
Refitted UMAPs are explanatory plots, not a model-selection metric. Continuous
liquid substructure need not form discrete thermodynamic phases.

The static assay is transductive for GeoFrame. Generating potentials of static
snapshots are unknown. The 45-root future corpus and 15 development roots are reused;
it is not a new final test. Ta/Zr ordered-liquid proxies remain candidates without
validated future fate. Cross-model associations are descriptive and confounded
by input/training differences; within-run factorial contrasts are more informative.
